# ACCOUNTABILLIES — Higgsfield AI Hyper Prompt Pack

A promo-video prompt set for the webcam pose-matching game (scan → outline
rush → snap → score → elimination). Paste the **Master Hyper Prompt** for a
single hero spot, or run the **Shot Deck** shot-by-shot and cut them together.

---

## 1. MASTER HYPER PROMPT (single paste)

> **ACCOUNTABILLIES // "FIT THE FUTURE"** — 16:9 cinematic, 10s, hyper-real
> near-future consumer-tech spot, shot on anamorphic 35mm with volumetric haze.
>
> A pitch-black minimalist apartment lit only by the cyan bloom of a phone
> propped on a tripod. A young athlete stands mid-room. A **holographic
> scanning lattice** blooms outward from the phone and wraps their body — 33
> glowing skeletal landmarks igniting one by one down the limbs, thin data
> tethers measuring shoulder width, arm span, femur length, floor plane. Live
> HUD numerals flicker in the air beside each measurement. The lattice snaps
> shut with a hard white flash: **LOCK.**
>
> Camera whips 180° around them (**Robo Arm orbit**, slight dutch) as a
> **human-shaped hand-drawn outline** ignites at a vanishing point deep behind
> them — tiny, hazy, cool-tinted, glowing soft blue — and **rushes forward on a
> perspective curve** like a wall in Hole in the Wall, scaling from pinprint to
> life-size, resolving from smeared haze into crisp searing white as it
> arrives. Its wobbly marker-drawn edges jitter and breathe, three-frame
> boil-animated, like ink alive on glass.
>
> A **gold landing ring** burns into the floor at the subject's feet.
> Countdown glyphs **3 — 2 — 1** stamp the air in chrome, each impact
> shockwaving the haze. The subject explodes into a Warrior III / standing-
> splits contortion, one leg airborne, arms crossed overhead, threading
> *exactly* through the negative space of the outline. **Time freezes.**
>
> **Bullet-time orbit** through the frozen instant: the camera arcs through the
> gap between body and outline while a **segmentation mask** peels off the
> subject as a luminous magenta shell — coverage and precision fields
> visualized as two overlapping volumes, their intersection blazing gold. A
> giant score numeral slams in: **97**. Confetti of light. Shutter snap. The
> frozen frame flattens into a **photo tile** that flies backward and lands in
> a vast floating **photo wall** — dozens of scored snapshots, each one a
> different impossible pose, receding into infinity.
>
> Hard cut to a rising red **ELIMINATION** bar; the outline shrinks, the timer
> shrinks, the poses get crueler — a rapid-fire montage of silhouettes:
> one-leg balances, frog crouches, sky-high kicks, mid-air jump poses caught at
> apex, each one snapping to a flash and a rising target number 45 → 80.
>
> Final beat: subject lands, breathless, grinning, drenched in cyan and gold
> rim light. Logo resolves in hand-drawn marker stroke over chrome:
> **ACCOUNTABILLIES.** Tagline etches in beneath: *"Your body is the
> controller."*
>
> **Style:** near-future sports-tech commercial, Nike x Apple Vision energy,
> deep blacks, cyan/magenta/gold triad, volumetric god rays, motion-blurred
> particles, holographic UI overlays, anamorphic lens flares, shallow depth of
> field, 24fps with 120fps ramp on the freeze, film grain, high contrast.
> **Camera:** robo-arm orbit → crash zoom → bullet time → whip pan → dolly out.
> **Audio:** accelerating tick-tock, heartbeat sub-bass, rising panic whine,
> hard shutter snap on every score, one triumphant synth swell at the logo.
> **Negative:** no text artifacts, no distorted hands, no extra limbs, no
> flat lighting, no stock-footage look, no watermark.
